How it works

  1. You get ₦500,000 practice credits. Free, not real money.
  2. Buy slots in any business. Each slot is a small piece of its daily earnings.
  3. Come back every real day to collect. Some days good, some days na loss, like real life.
  4. Sell slots back anytime at the current valuation. It moves up and down.
  5. Track your P&L, top up or withdraw practice credits in Account. None of it is real money.
  6. Grow your net worth and climb the board.
