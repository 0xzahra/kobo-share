What Kobo Share is and isn't (and how it differs from Rakuten Kobo and Kobo360)

Short answer: Kobo Share is a free, practice-only simulator that shows how owning slices of Nigerian small businesses works, using practice credits and fictional example businesses. It is not an investment, not a crowdfunding platform, and no real money is used, collected or paid.

What is Kobo Share not?

It is not an investment offer or solicitation, not a broker or crowdfunding portal, not registered with SEC Nigeria, and does not give financial advice. Practice scores are simulated and nothing is guaranteed.

Is Kobo Share related to Rakuten Kobo?

No. Rakuten Kobo is an e-book and e-reader company. Kobo Share has no connection to it.

Is Kobo Share related to Kobo360?

No. Kobo360 is a logistics and haulage company. Kobo Share has no connection to it. "Kobo" here refers to the kobo, the smaller unit of the Nigerian naira.

Who is Kobo Share for?

Curious learners and small-business owners who want to understand fractional ownership and business numbers before ever dealing with real platforms. You can open the simulator without signing in.
