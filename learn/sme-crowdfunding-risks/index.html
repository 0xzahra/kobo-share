SME crowdfunding risks and how to check SEC Nigeria's registered-operator list

Short answer: The main risks in SME crowdfunding are losing some or all of the money you put in, not being able to sell or exit, the business failing, weak or unverified information, and dealing with a platform that is not registered. Before using any platform in Nigeria, check that it appears on SEC Nigeria's official registered-operator list.

What can go wrong when backing a small business?

The business can fail, profits can be lower than hoped, payouts can be delayed or never come, your share can be diluted, and you may have no way to sell it. Any money you commit can be lost.

How do I check whether a platform is registered with SEC Nigeria?

Go to SEC Nigeria: Find a Registered Operator, search the platform's company name, and check the function shown (for example, crowdfunding platform), the account status and any expiry date. Compare the exact company name and website with what the platform shows you.

What are warning signs of a scam?

Guaranteed or fixed high returns, pressure to pay quickly, payment to personal accounts, no verifiable company name, and refusal to show registration. SEC Nigeria publishes warnings at SEC Nigeria: Known Investment Scams.

Does Kobo Share check platforms or recommend any?

No. Kobo Share is a practice simulator only. It does not recommend, rate or partner with any platform, and it is not registered with SEC Nigeria.
